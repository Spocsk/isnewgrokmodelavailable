## Direction contract

THESIS: The viewport is a riveted factory enamel status plate, in service or not. It refuses the hero-metric status dashboard (big number, tiny label, glow, cards).

OWN-WORLD: Vitreous Brunswick green fills the glass when the machine is idle; bone condensed grotesk stamps the answer; four corner rivets; idle plate is cold fired enamel. When the model exists the plate inhabits from within (vermilion field, same lettering). No cards, no chrome, no traffic-light green/red.

STORY: In one glance the visitor knows whether Grok 4.7 is on xAI’s catalogue. They can leave the tab open. Failure keeps the last stamp and says the catalogue is unreachable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed plate. The answer (OUI or NON) is the only large type, centered, occupying most of the plate. Directly under it, same family, much smaller: “Grok 4.7”. Bottom edge: relative check time. No label above the answer. Signature interaction: the field floods and the word restamps when state changes; while polling, a single rivet breathes.
